WebGazer demo

This is an unmodified build of WebGazer — ridge regression on 60×60 grayscale eye patches, self-calibrated from your clicks. No head-pose correction.

Click each of the 9 calibration dots a few times while looking directly at them. Then look around — the red marker WebGazer draws is its gaze prediction. Keep clicking around the page to keep it learning. Press h to show/hide this panel.
